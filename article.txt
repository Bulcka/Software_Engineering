Python is a popular programming language created by Guido van Rossum and first released in 1991. It is known for its simplicity and readability, making it a great choice for beginners and professionals alike. Python supports multiple programming paradigms, including procedural, object-oriented, and functional programming. It has a large and active community that constantly contributes to its development through libraries and frameworks. Some of the most popular Python frameworks include Django, Flask, and FastAPI. Python is also widely used in data science, artificial intelligence, and web development. Its versatility and ease of use make it one of the most powerful tools in modern programming.
